Our Academic Curriculum

At Giants Secondary School Bukinda, we offer a rigorous academic program structured under the UNEB curriculum for both Ordinary Level (O-Level) and Advanced Level (A-Level). We place a strong emphasis on continuous assessment, practical skills, and holistic mentorship.

O-Level (UCE Curriculum)

Our O-Level curriculum follows the lower secondary curriculum designed to nurture critical thinking, innovation, and practical problem-solving skills.


Core & Elective Subjects:

• Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology

• English Language, Literature in English

• History, Geography, Christian Religious Education (CRE)

• Agriculture, Entrepreneurship, Computer Studies / ICT

A-Level (UACE Curriculum)

Our Advanced Level program prepares students for university education and specialized career paths across Arts and Sciences.


Science Combinations:

• PCM, BCM, PEM, MEG, BCAG

Arts & Humanities Combinations:

• HEG, HEL, DEG, HED, LEG

Subsidiaries: General Paper (GP), Sub-Maths, Computer Studies (ICT)

Academic Support & Facilities

We provide students with the necessary infrastructure and guidance to maximize their performance in national examinations:


• 🔬 Modern Science & ICT Laboratories: Well-equipped science and computer labs for hands-on practical learning.

• 📚 Library & Resource Center: A vast collection of textbooks, past UNEB exam papers, and revision guides.

• ✍️ Regular Assessments: Weekly tests, mid-term examinations, and regional mock exams to prepare candidates for final UNEB papers.

• 👨‍🏫 Dedicated Teaching Staff: Experienced teachers committed to individual student mentoring and remedial classes.
