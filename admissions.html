Admissions Process

Giants Secondary School Bukinda welcomes applications from student candidates across Uganda and beyond. We admit students into Senior One (S.1), Senior Two (S.2), Senior Three (S.3), and Senior Five (S.5) based on academic merit, discipline, and commitment to learning.

O-Level Admission (S.1 - S.3)

Applicants seeking entry into lower secondary must present the following requirements during registration:


• 📄 Original Primary Leaving Examinations (PLE) Result Slip / Testimonial

• 📝 Recommendation Letter from the previous primary school

• 🖼️ Two recent passport-size photographs of the student

• 📋 Copy of the student's Birth Certificate or National Identification Number (NIN)

A-Level Admission (S.5)

Candidates applying for Advanced Level must satisfy UNEB cut-off requirements for their desired subject combinations:


• 📄 Official Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) Result Slip / Testimonial

• 📝 Letter of Conduct / Recommendation from the former school

• 🖼️ Two passport-size photos

• 📈 Verified subject combination selection form

Online Student Registration Form

Fill out the form below to apply online directly. Our admissions office will review your details and contact you.


Offline Application Steps

If you prefer to submit physical forms, follow these steps:


1. Obtain Application Form: Download the printable PDF application form or collect a copy from the school bursar's office in Bukinda.

2. Complete & Submit Documents: Attach all required academic documents, passport photos, and identification copies.

3. Entrance Interview / Assessment: Candidate interviews and verification of documents are conducted at the school premises.

4. Receive Admission Letter: Successful applicants will be issued an official admission letter alongside the requirements and fee structure.
