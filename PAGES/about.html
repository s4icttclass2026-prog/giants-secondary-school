About Our School

Building a foundation of integrity, discipline, and academic excellence.

Who We Are

Giants Secondary School Bukinda is a premier educational institution committed to delivering quality secondary education. Located in Bukinda, our school provides a holistic learning environment where students excel in national examinations while developing strong leadership skills, spiritual values, public speaking, and athletic talent.

Our Vision

To be a leading center of educational excellence, producing self-reliant, disciplined, and morally upright leaders for tomorrow.

Our Mission

To provide quality, affordable education and foster character development through innovative teaching, mentorship, and practical skills.

Core Values

Discipline, Academic Excellence, Integrity, Hard Work, God-fearing Values, and Community Responsibility.

In Loving & Eternal Memory

"Gone from our sight, but forever rooted in our hearts."

Late Mr. Agaba Innocent
Founding Executive Director

With heart-wrenching sorrow and heavy grief, the entire fraternity of Giants Secondary School Bukinda mourns the sudden and tragic loss of our founding Executive Director, the Late Mr. Agaba Innocent. His untimely passing in a fatal road accident has left an unimaginable void in our institution, our community, and the countless lives he touched.

Mr. Agaba Innocent was not just a leader; he was the pillar, visionary, and beating heart of our school. Through his unwavering sacrifice, wisdom, and deep fatherly love for every student, he laid the very foundation upon which Giants S.S. Bukinda stands today. He poured his life into building a sanctuary of quality education, moral uprightness, and hope for our children.

Though our hearts are deeply broken and wounded by his departure, his legacy of humility, passion, and selfless service will never be extinguished. We hold fast to the values he instilled in us and honor the indelible mark he left on our souls.

May God Almighty comfort his family, strengthen our school fraternity, and grant his soul eternal peace. Amen.
