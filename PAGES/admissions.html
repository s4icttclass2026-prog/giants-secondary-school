Admission Requirements

Senior 1 (S.1) Entry:

• PLE Result Slip / Mock Examination Results

• Recommendation letter from primary school

• Copy of Birth Certificate

• 2 Passport-size photographs


Senior 5 (S.5) Entry:

• UCE Result Slip / Identity Verification

• Recommendation letter from previous secondary school

• 2 Passport-size photographs

School Uniform Code

Ordinary Level (O-Level):

• Short-sleeved white shirt

• Grey trousers

• Official school badge & tie

• Black leather shoes with white socks


Advanced Level (A-Level):

• Long-sleeved white shirt

• Green trousers

• Official school badge & tie

• Black leather shoes with white socks
