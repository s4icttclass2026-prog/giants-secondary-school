Academic Programs

Nurturing intellectual growth through standard O-Level and A-Level curricula.

Ordinary Level (O-Level)

We offer a comprehensive lower-secondary curriculum focused on core competencies, science, human sciences, and practical skills.


Core Subjects Include:

Mathematics, English Language, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History, Geography, Entrepreneurship, and Computer Studies.

Advanced Level (A-Level)

Our upper-secondary program prepares students for university entry with specialized Arts and Science subject combinations.


Popular Combinations:

Sciences: PCM, PCB, BCM, MEG (with Subsidiary ICT/Math)

Arts: HEG, LEG, DEG, HEL (with General Paper)
